About Us

Practical software, built around everyday needs.

Focused software, without unnecessary complexity

Marbalux creates applications that help people organize their work, manage everyday tasks and make better use of their digital workspace. We focus on practical features that serve a clear purpose and are easy to understand.

OUR PURPOSE

Mission

Make everyday workflows simpler through useful software that gives people more control over their time, tasks and workspace.

LOOKING AHEAD

Vision

Grow a family of practical applications that adapt to the way people work and to the platforms they use, while keeping a clear purpose at the heart of every product.

WHAT GUIDES US

Values

Usefulness

Start with real needs and build features that make everyday work easier.

Simplicity

Keep interactions understandable and avoid adding complexity without a clear benefit.

Respect for privacy

Consider privacy when designing features and explain how each application handles information.

Continuous improvement

Learn from everyday use and feedback, and refine our applications with care.
