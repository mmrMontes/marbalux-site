REAL-TIME DESKTOPS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy describes data handling for Real-Time Desktops and support for this application. Publisher: Marbalux. For contact information, consult the Real-Time Desktops listing on Microsoft Store.

Information we access

Real-Time Desktops accesses information about Windows virtual desktops and windows, including their names, identifiers, positions and visual content, to display an overview. Previews and captures may contain personal information visible in other applications.

How information is used

Desktop background and taskbar captures are kept in memory while the app runs. The preview feature does not save these captures as image files or send them from the app to a server. The app does not include advertising or remote usage analytics.

Preferences and technical logs

The app stores local preferences such as language, shortcut, refresh intervals, animations and tutorial visibility. It also creates limited technical logs of startup, shutdown, errors and desktop switching. Logs may include timestamps, process and desktop identifiers, card indices and operation results. Rename diagnostics do not record the name text being entered. Error messages may contain technical details about the environment.

Data retention and local storage

In the desktop distribution, these files are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\RealTimeDesktops. For MSIX, Windows may redirect data to the package's private storage. App logs rotate by size. Preferences remain until the user resets or removes them. The MSI may preserve preferences after uninstalling; MSIX data handling depends on the Windows removal process.

Data sharing and Microsoft Store

The app's desktop and taskbar preview feature processes captures locally and does not transmit them to a server. Acquisition of the Microsoft Store edition is handled by Microsoft. Marbalux does not request card details inside Real-Time Desktops. Microsoft Store, Windows and the email provider are also subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

Support

If you contact support, we use your email address, message and any files you choose to send to respond and troubleshoot the issue. Messages may be stored by the email service used to handle support. You can request information or deletion at the contact address, subject to applicable obligations. Logs are not sent to support automatically.

Your choices

You can close the app from the system tray, change its preferences or uninstall it through Windows Settings. The app does not request camera, microphone or location permissions to display desktops. For questions about your data or its deletion, use the support contact information provided in the app's Microsoft Store listing.
